Features

What the beta includes

Team booking pages

Coming in beta

Create a team, invite members, and share a team booking link. Guests pick a time that works for the people who need to be there.

Round-robin and collective

Coming in beta

Round-robin: a meeting is assigned to one host, with optional fixed hosts always included. Collective: every required host has to be free, and all of them are added to the booking.

Reminders, insights, and SSO

Coming in beta

Email reminders before and after a meeting, a simple team dashboard (bookings, cancellations, no-shows, and how round-robin is splitting), and optional OIDC SSO. SSO is included in self-host. On the hosted product it sits on the Business plan. SMS reminders will be available as a usage-based add-on once a provider is chosen; there is no public SMS price yet.

FAQ

Licence, hosting, and timing

What licence applies?

This website is MIT. Our add-on packages are licensed AGPL-3.0. The Cal.diy snapshot they build on stays MIT.

Is self-host free, and is it complete?

Self-host is free and includes the full add-on, SSO included. That covers teams, round-robin, collective, reminder workflows, insights, and OIDC SSO, with community support. The image and docs are not out yet.

Who owns the data?

Self-host means your database. On the hosted product, the data is yours and exportable.

Is this affiliated with Cal.com?

No. SeatRota is an independent project that works with Cal.diy.

When will the beta be ready?

No dates are promised. The self-host image and docs come first. The hosted beta follows, when sign-up opens. Join the waitlist and we will mail you when each one is ready.
