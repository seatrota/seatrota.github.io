09:00Brief

Open-source Teams add-on for Cal.diy

Team scheduling for Cal.diy

Round-robin and collective team booking, on top of the Cal.diy you already run. Self-host is free. A hosted plan is on the way.

Join the waitlist → What’s in it

Cal.diy is the MIT community edition of a popular scheduling app. Its team features were removed in April 2026. SeatRota puts them back as an add-on you can run yourself.

10:30Features

Three things your team rota needs, back in Cal.diy.

  1. 01

    Team booking pages

    Coming in beta

    One booking page for the whole team, served from the Cal.diy instance you already run. People choose a time. SeatRota decides who takes it.

  2. 02

    Round-robin and collective scheduling

    Coming in beta

    Round-robin

    Each booking goes to one host, taking turns across the team. You can add fixed hosts who join every booking.

    Collective

    A time is only offered when every required host is free. When someone books it, all of them are added.

  3. 03

    Reminders, insights and SSO

    Coming in beta

    Reminders
    Workflows that remind hosts and attendees before a booking.
    Insights
    See how bookings are spread across the team.
    OIDC SSO
    Free when you self-host. Part of the Business plan when hosted.

15:30FAQ

Questions

What’s the licence?

The SeatRota add-on is AGPL-3.0. The Cal.diy base stays MIT, exactly as it is today.

Can I self-host it?

Yes. Self-hosting is free and includes every feature, SSO too. SeatRota runs on top of the Cal.diy instance you already operate.

Who owns the data?

You do. When you self-host, bookings and team data stay in your own database, on servers you control.

Is this affiliated with Cal.com?

No, it’s an independent project.

When can I use it?

Everything on this page is coming in the beta. The hosted plan follows after that. Join the waitlist and we’ll tell you when each one opens.
